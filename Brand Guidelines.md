# **White Cane AI Consulting — Brand Guidelines**

One page to answer: which logo, which colour, which font, where. If something is not covered here, keep it simple and use Obsidian Slate on Soft Silver with one Vivid Crimson accent.

## **1\. Logo versions**

| Version | What it is | Use it for |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Stacked** | Icon above, text below | Slide covers, posters, social images, square spaces |
| **Horizontal** | Icon left, text right | Default. Website header, email signature, documents, letterhead |
| **Icon** | Only the cane in the rounded square | Favicon, app icon, avatars, small spaces, watermark |
| **Wordmark** | Only the text | Footers, partner/sponsor strips, where the icon is already shown |

### **Colour versions**

| Version | Use on |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Color** | White or light backgrounds (Soft Silver, Misty Blue) |
| **White** | Dark backgrounds (Obsidian Slate, Midnight Sapphire) and photos |
| **Black** | One-colour printing, stamps, engraving, black & white documents |

Note: the cane in the logo files is transparent, so it takes the colour of the background. Never place the **Color** version on a dark background: the cane will disappear. Use the **White** version instead.

### **Clear space and minimum size**

> * **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the red dot, on all four sides. No text or images inside that space.  
> * **Minimum size:** Icon 24 px (8 mm in print). Horizontal logo 120 px wide (35 mm in print). Stacked logo 80 px wide (25 mm in print).

### **Don't**

> * Don't stretch or squash the logo. Always resize from a corner with Shift held.  
> * Don't change the logo colours or recolour parts of it.  
> * Don't add shadows, outlines, glows or other effects.  
> * Don't rotate the logo or change the position of the icon and text.  
> * Don't place the logo on busy photos without a dark or light overlay behind it.  
> * Don't retype the logo text. Always use the logo files.

## **3\. Colours**

### **Accessibility (important for a brand named White Cane)**

> * **Vivid Crimson is for large or bold text only** (headings, buttons, highlighted words). On Soft Silver or with white text, its contrast is too low for small body text.  
> * **Button text** on Vivid Crimson: white, bold, at least 16 px. For small buttons use Garnet Wine as the background instead.  
> * **Body text** always Obsidian Slate or Midnight Sapphire. Slate Harbor only for secondary text, never below 14 px.  
> * **Never rely on colour alone** to carry meaning (for example in charts, add labels or patterns).  
> * Plum Velvet and Rosewood Blush are decorative. Don't use them for text.

## **4\. Typography**

| Font | Role | Where to get it | Fallback |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Lilex** | Main brand font: headings, website, slides, tech accents | Free, open source (GitHub: mishamyrt/Lilex) | Consolas, Courier New |
| **Noto Sans** | Logo text; body text in long documents | Free, Google Fonts | Arial |

**Why two fonts:** Lilex is a monospace font with a strong technical character, ideal for headings and short text. For long paragraphs (reports, proposals, long web pages), Noto Sans is easier to read. Rule: Lilex for anything short and visible, Noto Sans for anything long.

### **Sizes**

| Level | Website | Documents (Word/Docs) | Slides | Font |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| H1 | 48 px Bold | 24 pt Bold | 40 pt Bold | Lilex |
| H2 | 32 px SemiBold | 18 pt SemiBold | 28 pt SemiBold | Lilex |
| H3 | 24 px Medium | 14 pt Medium | 22 pt Medium | Lilex |
| Body | 17 px Regular, line height 1.6 | 11 pt Regular, line spacing 1.3 | 18 pt Regular | Noto Sans |
| Small / captions | 14 px | 9 pt | 14 pt | Noto Sans |
| Labels, tags, buttons | 14–16 px, uppercase, letter-spacing \+5% | — | 14 pt | Lilex |

**PowerPoint / Word tip:** Lilex is not installed on most clients' computers. When sending editable files, embed fonts (File → Options → Save → Embed fonts) or send as PDF.
